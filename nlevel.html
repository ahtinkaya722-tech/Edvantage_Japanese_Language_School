What Is JLPT?

The Japanese-Language Proficiency Test is an international exam that measures Japanese ability from beginner level N5 to advanced level N1. EDVANTAGE classes help students build vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening, and test confidence.

Class Levels

Choose Your JLPT Level

Start from the right level and move forward with a clear study path.

N5

Beginner Foundation

Hiragana, Katakana, basic grammar, daily vocabulary, simple reading, and listening practice.

N4

Basic Communication

Essential sentence patterns, conversation practice, reading short passages, and exam drills.

N3

Intermediate Japanese

Stronger grammar, kanji growth, longer reading, listening strategies, and timed practice.

N2

Upper Intermediate

Advanced grammar, natural expressions, speed reading, vocabulary building, and mock tests.

N1

Advanced Mastery

Complex reading, advanced listening, formal expressions, exam strategy, and intensive revision.
